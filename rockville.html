Trail Stop No. 5

Rockville Chapel

242 Pleasant St, Millis, MA 02054 · Dedicated 1877

Dedicated in 1877 and located at 242 Pleasant Street, the Rockville Chapel served the small mill village of Rockville, a 19th-century industrial community along the Charles River. The chapel offered worship space for workers and their families and remains a modest but enduring landmark of Millis's industrial-era heritage.
