About the Project

An Eagle Scout Service Project · Troop 15

The Millis Historic Trail is an Eagle Scout Service Project created by Ben Vaccaro of Troop 15, Boy Scouts of America. The trail connects ten of the most significant historic sites in Millis, Massachusetts, inviting residents and visitors to walk through more than three centuries of local history.

First settled in 1657, the land that became Millis was originally part of Dedham and later granted to Medfield. Known for generations as East Medway, the community separated from Medway on February 24, 1885 to form the independent town of Millis, named for railroad man and town founder Lansing Millis.

Each stop on the trail carries a sign with a QR code that links to its page on this site, so visitors can read the history of a site while standing in front of it. To learn more about the town's broader story, visit the Town History page, or begin walking from the Trail Map.

How to use the trail

The ten stops can be walked in any order. Each page lists the site's address, date, and ownership, a short history with sources, a photo gallery, and walking directions. Several sites are private residences; please view those respectfully from the public way.
