Trail Stop No. 9

South End Pond

Orchard Street, Millis, MA 02054 · c. 1676

Tucked off South End Pond Road, this quiet pond figures in local tradition tied to King Philip's War (c. 1676), when nearby woodlands witnessed conflict between English settlers and the Wampanoag. Generations of Millis residents have passed down stories of “King Philip's Trees” marking the area, blending documented history with living local memory.
