Trail Stop No. 4

Prospect Hill Cemetery

74 Auburn Rd, Millis, MA 02054 · Est. 1714

Founded in 1714 at 74 Auburn Road, Prospect Hill Cemetery is among the oldest burial grounds in Millis. It covers more than 18 acres and holds over 2,800 burials, and was listed on the National Register of Historic Places on March 15, 2006.[1]

The older section is laid out in a horseshoe-shaped bowl with terraced slopes and mature tree plantings, while the newer 1932 addition holds more uniform granite markers, with the northeastern portion reserved for military veterans. A granite step and commemorative boulder mark the site of the town's first meeting house.[1]

Notable burials include Christian Herter, U.S. Secretary of State and Governor of Massachusetts; Medal of Honor recipients Charles Church Roberts and William D. Newland; and Charles Wesley Emerson, founder of Emerson College in Boston.[1]
