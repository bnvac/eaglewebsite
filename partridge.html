Trail Stop No. 2

John Partridge House

315 Exchange St, Millis, MA 02054 · Built c. 1659

Standing at 315 Exchange Street, the John Partridge House is one of the oldest surviving residences in Millis. The home reflects the modest center-chimney New England farmhouse style typical of the colonial period and remains a tangible link to the Partridge family, among the area's earliest settlers.
