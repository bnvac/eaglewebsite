Trail Stop No. 7

Ellice School

185 Pleasant St, Millis, MA 02054 · Built 1849

Built in 1849 at 185 Pleasant Street, the Ellice School served generations of local children in the one-room schoolhouse tradition common to mid-19th-century New England. Buildings like this were the heart of district-school education before consolidated public schools were established later in the century.
