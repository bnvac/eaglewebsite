Trail Stop No. 8

Oak Grove Park

444 Exchange St, Millis, MA 02054 · Est. 1714

Oak Grove Farm takes its name from Lansing Millis, the man this town was named after in 1885. At its peak one of the largest dairy farms in New England, the property was acquired by the Town of Millis in 1984 and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Today its 108 acres of fields and trails remain open to the public year-round.
