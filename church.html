Trail Stop No. 1

Church of Christ

142 Exchange St, Millis, MA 02054 · Est. 1714

Located at 142 Exchange Street, the Church of Christ traces its origins to 1714, when the Second Parish of Medfield was established to serve families on the west side of the Charles River. The meetinghouse has anchored the town's religious and civic life for over three centuries.
